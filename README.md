# NextStep Bay

**Skill-matched job transition finder for Bay Area workers facing job displacement.**

🔗 **Live demo:** https://tiasaini11-hub.github.io/nextstep-bay/

## The Problem

Job displacement — from automation, AI, and economic shifts — is accelerating. Most transition/reskilling tools give generic, national-level advice. Workers are left without a clear, local next step.

## What It Does

NextStep Bay takes two inputs:
- **Current or most recent job** (retail, warehouse, driver, food service, etc.)
- **Bay Area county**

And returns:
1. **2 skill-matched next roles** — jobs that build on the skills the person already has, not a random career reset
2. **Growth outlook + retraining time** for each role
3. **A real local training program** to get started (e.g. City College of SF, GRID Alternatives, Laney College)
4. **County-specific workforce resources** for broader support

## Why It's Different

- **Hyper-local**: real Bay Area programs and county resources, not generic national links
- **Skills-first**: matches people to roles that build on what they already know, lowering the barrier to transition
- **Zero friction**: no login, no forms beyond two dropdowns — usable by anyone in under a minute

## Tech

Single-page static web app — HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, no frameworks or backend required. Deployed via GitHub Pages.

## Future Improvements

- Real-time labor market data (BLS / EDD APIs) instead of curated dataset
- Expand job categories and county coverage across the Bay Area
- Add resume/skills upload for more personalized matching
- Partner directly with workforce boards for warm handoff / application tracking
